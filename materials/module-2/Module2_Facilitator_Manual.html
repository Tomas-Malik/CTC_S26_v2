Module 2: Scattered Sources

From Data Dump to Decision-Ready — everything you need to know before you teach this, including a full walkthrough of what's actually in the data.

90 minutes Level 1 Companion to Module 1

Overview

Learners play a team member at Pinnacle Peak Home Services who inherits a 12-tab, undocumented spreadsheet folder and is asked to produce a Q3 report against expectations by 2pm — with no one around who understands the file. They work the same messy folder twice: once with AI as an assistant (they stay hands-on, AI orients and suggests), once with AI as an analyst (AI drives more of the work directly, so verification becomes the job). Same data, two very different levels of trust.

This is a direct sequel to Module 1's "template vs. no template" distinction — here the axis is autonomy, not example-availability. Module 1 is not a hard prerequisite, but the vocabulary (uncover your goal, one step at a time, don't outsource your thinking) is reused and assumed familiar.

Preparation

  1. Read the Data Notes section below in full before you open the deck — it's the fastest way to get oriented without digging through all six source files yourself.
  2. Skim the deck's speaker notes for timing and beat-by-beat guidance.
  3. Memorize the one chain of numbers that ties everything together (below) — it's the answer key for almost every diagnostic check in Beats One and Two.
  4. Nothing needs printing. Have Pinnacle_Peak_Q3_Data.xlsx and Q2_Checkin_FINAL_FINAL_v3.pptx open in separate windows, ready to share-screen if a learner wants to see them live.
  5. Confirm learners have spreadsheet software available (Excel or Google Sheets) — Beats One and Two both require opening an actual multi-tab workbook, not just reading text off a slide.

The one chain to know cold

$261,500 — the correct Q3 2023 baseline, per Sandra's official Q3 2023 Performance Report (Pinnacle_Peak_Q3_2023_Report.pdf) and the "Q3 2023 Archive" tab.

→ ×1.15 — Director Chen's FY2024 growth target, announced company-wide January 15, 2024 (Email_Chain_FY2024_Targets.pdf).

= $300,725 — the resulting Q3 2024 target, confirmed independently in both the email thread and the Q2 check-in's own targets table.

The Ottawa pilot (CAD figures) is explicitly excluded from all of the above — confirmed by both Sandra's email and the Q2 check-in.

Data Notes — what's in each file

You don't need to open every tab yourself before class, but you should know what's below so you can recognize when a learner (or their AI) gets something right or wrong.

XLSXPinnacle_Peak_Q3_Data.xlsx — the 12-tab file, core material for both Beats

  • Dashboard — literally says "KEY METRIC PLACEHOLDER," flags its own formulas as broken. Intentionally unfinished; Sandra never got to it. Still worth skimming first — it's what anyone opens first in real life.
  • Monthly Revenue — this quarter's numbers by month/service line, with a note flagging a July system migration and pointing to the Archive tab for the prior-year comparison.
  • Q3 2023 Archive — CORRECT the real prior-year baseline ($261,500 total). Independently confirmed by the official Q3 2023 report and the email thread.
  • OLD – do not use — TRAP gross revenue before refunds, ~8% higher than the real number. This is the exact tab that caused the Q2 report's v2 mistake — the built-in "will you repeat history" test.
  • Regional Breakdown — TRAP (subtle) states a Q3 2023 total of $261,800 — but its own four regional line items ($87,000 + $73,500 + $68,000 + $33,000) actually sum to $261,500. The tab's own total row doesn't reconcile with its own rows above it. Most learners will just notice "two different totals exist somewhere"; a sharp group might find the deeper bug.
  • Target vs Actual — Q4 2024 is marked TBD. Reporting it as an actual number is a fabrication, not a rounding error.
  • Ottawa Pilot (CAD) — explicitly labeled Canadian dollars, explicitly excluded from company-wide targets. Sits right next to USD tabs with no other warning.
  • Service Tickets / Technician Performance / Customer Satisfaction / Headcount / Expenses — supporting operational detail. Mostly clean, with small caveats (ratings "manually entered this quarter," blank complaint cells meaning no complaint, not missing data).

XLSXPinnacle_Peak_Revenue_2023 / 2024 / 2025 / 2026_H1.xlsx — four-year trend data, mainly for Beat Two

  • Clean monthly/quarterly series, useful for a trend chart in the Beat Two report.
  • 2023 & 2024 files cross-reference the Q3_Data workbook exactly — both state the Q3 2023 total as $261,500, agreeing with the Archive tab (not OLD, not Regional Breakdown's total).
  • 2025 file — TRAP its own notes state "all figures are management estimates," not actuals. Easy to accidentally chart alongside real actuals as if equivalent.
  • 2026 H1 file — flags "Cleaning softness... possible East region competitor impact" as an open, unresolved question — a live "Needs a Decision"-style item if a learner wants to extend the trend analysis.

PPTXQ2_Checkin_FINAL_FINAL_v3.pptx — last quarter's real report, the Beat Two template

  • Speaker notes explicitly document that v2 accidentally used the OLD tab for HVAC revenue. This is the exact mistake Beat Two's diagnostic asks learners to check for.
  • Its own targets table is where the $300,725 Q3 2024 target is stated plainly — useful to point to if a learner asks "where does this number even come from."
  • "Needs a Decision" equivalent: whether the Ottawa pilot counts toward Q3 targets — still open at the time of the Q2 check-in.

PDFEmail_Chain_FY2024_Targets.pdf — 11-message thread, reverse chronological

  • Newest message is at the top — you have to read to the bottom to find Director Chen's original announcement (Jan 15) and trace the confirmation chain forward.
  • Contains Sandra's explicit written instruction: use the Archive tab, not OLD.
  • Good optional extension for Beat One: a mini scavenger hunt for where the 15% target actually comes from, if you want to stretch the orientation activity.

PDFPinnacle_Peak_Q3_2023_Report.pdf — Sandra's official Q3 2023 report, the ground-truth source

  • The authoritative source for the $261,500 baseline, and for the 15%-growth math that produces the $300,725 Q3 2024 target.
  • Your answer if a learner asks "how do we actually know $261,500 is the right number" — this is the primary source, not a spreadsheet tab.

Facilitator judgment calls

A few things worth deciding ahead of time, since the module doesn't hand you a single fixed answer:

  • How far to let Beat One's exploration run. 12 minutes is enough to catch the OLD/Archive conflict and the currency issue for most groups, but not always the Regional Breakdown sub-total bug. That's fine — it's a bonus find, not a required one.
  • Whether to introduce the email chain and PDF report at all. They're not required for the core exercise (the 12-tab file and Q2 check-in are enough), but they're there if you want a group to go one level deeper on "how do we know what's true here."
  • How hard to push on the Sandra/no-handoff-doc thread. It's a real operational risk in the scenario, not just flavor text — worth treating with the same weight as Module 1's Denise situation, not as background color.
