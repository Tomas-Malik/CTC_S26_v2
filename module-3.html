AI Literacy 101 · Module 3

Data On Demand

From Deck to Dashboard: turning a folder that keeps growing into an answer that’s always current, with AI as assistant or analyst.

90 minutes Level 1 Who it’s for: everyone

TL;DR

You’ll pick the chart that actually answers a question, then build a living dashboard version of it from a folder that keeps growing — once using AI as an assistant to choose and check the visual, once letting AI build the self-updating tool directly. Same data as Module 2, one new twist: the report never really finishes, so you stop rebuilding it.

Prerequisites Computer literacy. Module 2 recommended — this module continues its Pinnacle Peak scenario directly and reuses the AI as assistant / AI as analyst framing.
What you’ll need A laptop with internet access, spreadsheet software (Excel or Google Sheets), and access to an AI assistant (ChatGPT or Claude).
Who it’s for Everyone — no technical background required.

After this module, you’ll be able to

  • 1 Pick the chart that answers the question — not the one the tool defaults to; trend, comparison, and composition are three different questions, and three different charts.
  • 2 Build the thing once, not the report every month — turn a repeated task into something that already has next month’s answer in it.
  • 3 Catch what a living dashboard gets wrong — so you don’t oversell what an AI-built tool can and can’t tell you.

Preparation

  • 1 Skim the deck’s speaker notes for timing and beat-by-beat guidance — there’s no separate facilitator manual for this module yet, so the notes are the full guide.
  • 2 Familiarity with Module 2 is recommended, not required — Module 3 opens with “Last time, on Pinnacle Peak…” and assumes that scenario context.
  • 3 Review the axis-honesty numbers before teaching: HVAC revenue grew from $124,500 to $145,100, Q3 2023 to Q3 2024 — about 16% real growth. That’s the reveal behind the “why charts lie” activity early in the deck.
  • 4 Nothing to print. The four revenue-trend files are the “growing folder” for the hands-on activity — have them ready to share-screen or distribute digitally.
  • 5 Confirm learners have spreadsheet software and AI assistant access sorted, same logistics as Modules 1 and 2.
